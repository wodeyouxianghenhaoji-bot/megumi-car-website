Japan Quality Exports is a global motor vehicle export company specializing in high quality used vehicles sourced directly from Japan. The company connects international buyers with dealer stock, auctions, and private inventory while managing the entire export process, from sourcing and inspection to documentation, shipping, and global logistics.

Backed by extensive automotive industry experience and a trusted network of suppliers, Japan Quality Exports provides transparent, reliable, and professional service to dealers and individual buyers worldwide.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does the shipping process take?

Shipping times depend on the destination country, shipping schedule, port availability and customs procedures. Our team can provide an estimated shipping timeframe when preparing your quotation.

Do you handle export paperwork?

Yes. We assist with the export documentation and shipping arrangements required for the vehicle export process from Japan.

Can I request a specific vehicle?

Yes. If you cannot find the vehicle you are looking for in our current inventory, contact us with your preferred make, model, year, budget and other requirements.

Request a Vehicle
Can I buy from Japanese auctions?

Yes. Eligible customers can access our auction vehicle section and request assistance with purchasing vehicles available through Japanese auctions. 

What countries do you ship to?

We provide export and shipping support for customers in many countries worldwide. Contact us with your destination country and preferred vehicle so we can provide the available options.

What do I need to do after the vehicle arrives?

After arrival, you will need to complete the customs clearance, import and registration procedures required by your country. Requirements vary by destination.

Please review our Import Guides or contact your local authorities for country-specific requirements.
